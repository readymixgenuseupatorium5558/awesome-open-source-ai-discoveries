# Chatterbox: Prototype narration or a speaking character using a voice you own or have permission to use

A family of text-to-speech models with expressive controls, multilingual options and reference-audio voice conditioning.

## Demo or examples

[Explore the creator’s demo or examples](https://huggingface.co/spaces/ResembleAI/Chatterbox)

Official Chatterbox demo linked by upstream; hosted availability may vary and compute may queue. Other model variants have separate demos.
